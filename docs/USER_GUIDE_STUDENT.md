# Student User Guide — FNU Web Portal

Welcome to the FNU student portal. This guide walks you through every task you'll complete online — applying, enrolling, paying, tracking your results, and getting help.

Portal URL: **https://portal.fnu.ac.fj**

---

## 1. Applying to FNU

You do **not** need an account to apply.

1. Go to **https://portal.fnu.ac.fj/apply**.
2. Complete the five-step application form:
   - **Personal details** — legal name, date of birth, citizenship, contact info.
   - **Prior qualifications** — your secondary school or previous tertiary results.
   - **Programme choice** — pick your first and second preference, and your preferred campus.
   - **Documents** — upload your academic transcripts, national ID, and any supporting evidence. Accepted formats: PDF, JPG, PNG. Max 5 MB per file.
   - **Review & submit**.
3. You'll see a confirmation screen with your **application number** (e.g. `APP-2026-00142`). Save it.
4. You'll receive an email confirming submission. If it doesn't arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam folder.

### Tracking your application

Use the link in the confirmation email. The status progresses through:

- **Received** — we have it
- **Under review** — admissions staff is checking eligibility
- **Documents requested** — we need more from you; the email explains what
- **Offer made** — see next section
- **Declined** — with a reason

### Conditional offers for school leavers

If you're still completing Year 13, we may issue a **conditional offer** — an acceptance that becomes final once your final results meet the stated grade threshold. You'll be asked to upload your final transcript when results publish.

## 2. Accepting an offer

1. Open the offer link in your email. It goes to **`https://portal.fnu.ac.fj/offer/<token>`**.
2. Read the offer letter (PDF) — download a copy for your records.
3. Click **Accept** or **Decline**.
4. Offers expire after **7 days**. Accept before then or request an extension by replying to the email.

On acceptance, you'll be given your student ID and portal login credentials by email.

## 3. First login

1. Go to **https://portal.fnu.ac.fj/login**.
2. Enter your FNU email and the password from the offer-acceptance email.
3. You'll be prompted to change your password on first login.

**Forgot your password?** Use the "Forgot password" link on the login page. You'll get an email with a reset link valid for 1 hour.

**Account locked?** After 5 wrong passwords, your account locks for 15 minutes. Wait it out, or email registry@fnu.ac.fj.

## 4. The student dashboard

Your dashboard has six cards:

| Card | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Course Registration** | Pick your courses for the current semester |
| **My Timetable** | See when and where your classes meet; export to your calendar |
| **My Courses** | Your currently registered courses with Moodle links and lecturer contacts |
| **My Grades** | Results from past semesters, plus GPA |
| **Make a Payment** | Pay tuition via M-PAiSA, MyCash, or bank transfer |
| **Student ID** | Your digital ID card with QR verification |

An **outstanding-fees banner** appears at the top if you have overdue payments.

## 5. Course registration

1. Click **Course Registration**.
2. The portal shows your programme's core courses and available electives for the current semester.
3. Courses you don't qualify for show a reason — e.g. "ACC201 requires ACC101 (grade C or better)".
4. Tick the courses you want. The portal flags any **timetable clashes** as you go.
5. Check the **total credit points** matches your programme's per-semester load.
6. Click **Confirm registration**.
7. You'll see your new timetable and an **itemised invoice** — one line per course.

### Changing your registration

You can withdraw from or swap courses up to the add/drop deadline each semester. See the academic calendar on the FNU main site for exact dates. After the deadline, contact the registry (see §11).

## 6. Timetable and calendar export

**My Timetable** shows your weekly schedule. The **Export to calendar** button downloads a `.ics` file that imports cleanly into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook.

Subscribe (don't just import) if you want changes to sync automatically — re-download once per week.

## 7. Paying fees

1. Click **Make a Payment**.
2. Choose a method:
   - **M-PAiSA** — Vodafone mobile money. You'll be redirected to the M-PAiSA page to confirm.
   - **MyCash** — Digicel mobile money. Similar redirect flow.
   - **Bank transfer** — pay into FNU's nominated bank account. Upload the deposit slip; finance will mark your invoice paid within 2 business days.
3. Pay the **full balance** or a **custom amount** (e.g. an instalment).
4. If the gateway fails or times out, you can retry. Each attempt is recorded; you won't be double-charged.

### Payment plans

For tuition above a threshold, you can **set up an instalment plan** from the **Make a Payment** page. The portal generates a schedule (typically 3–4 payments). Reminders are sent 3 days before each due date. Falling two instalments behind risks de-registration — you'll get warnings first.

### Invoice PDFs

From **My Account** you can download any invoice as a PDF with the official FNU letterhead.

## 8. Digital student ID

Click **Student ID** on the dashboard.

- The card shows your photo, name, student ID number, programme, and QR code.
- To prove your identity on campus (e.g. at the library), show the card — security or staff scan the QR.
- The scanned QR takes them to a public verification page showing your photo, name, and programme only (no grades, no balance, no contact info).
- Cards are valid for 12 months and regenerate automatically.

### Updating your profile photo

From **My Account → Profile → Change photo**:

- JPG or PNG, max 2 MB.
- Head and shoulders, plain background, no sunglasses/caps.
- The portal resizes and strips metadata automatically.

## 9. Grades and transcripts

**My Grades** lists every completed course with:

- Grade letter and grade point
- Credits earned
- Semester and year
- **Cumulative GPA** and **semester GPA**

Grades appear once your lecturer has **finalised** the assessment period — usually within 2 weeks of the exam. If a grade seems wrong, contact your lecturer via the email on **My Courses** first; if unresolved, raise an enquiry (§11).

## 10. AI chatbot

Look for the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner of any authenticated page.

- Ask in plain English — e.g. "What's my balance?" "When is the add/drop deadline?" "How do I change campus?"
- The chatbot pulls from your own record (if you're logged in) and from FNU's FAQ.
- If it can't answer, it offers to **escalate** to registry staff — your question becomes an enquiry (§11).
- It won't share sensitive information with anyone but you.

## 11. Enquiries and support

### Raise an enquiry

If the chatbot can't help, or you prefer to talk to a human:

1. Click **Help** → **Contact registry** in the portal nav.
2. Choose a category (admissions, fees, academic, IT, other).
3. Write what you need; attach a screenshot if useful.
4. You'll get a reference number (e.g. `ENQ-2026-00342`).
5. Staff respond via email; you can see status in the portal.

When your enquiry is **resolved**, you'll be asked to **rate the support** (1–5 stars). Your feedback goes to the section head.

### Emergency contact

- **IT issues** (can't log in, portal error) — it-helpdesk@fnu.ac.fj or call +679 3394000.
- **Fees / payments** — finance@fnu.ac.fj.
- **Admissions** — registry@fnu.ac.fj.

## 12. Browser, device, and accessibility

- Works on **Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge** (current versions).
- Mobile-responsive on iOS and Android; you can complete every task on a phone.
- Keyboard navigation throughout; screen readers supported (WCAG 2.1 AA).
- If you find an accessibility issue, email it-helpdesk@fnu.ac.fj with "A11Y" in the subject.

## 13. Security hygiene

- **Never share your password.** FNU staff will never ask for it.
- Log out on shared computers (top-right menu → Sign out).
- If you suspect your account has been accessed by someone else, change your password immediately and email it-helpdesk@fnu.ac.fj.
